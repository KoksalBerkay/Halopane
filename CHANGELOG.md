# Changelog

## 0.1.1 (2026-11-18)

- YouTube opens as the desktop site. The mobile site kept loading posts and moving the page at the glasses' screen size. A saved choice for YouTube is kept.
- Fullscreen on desktop YouTube opens the video in Halopane's full-screen view.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-17)

First public version.

- A workspace of up to four browser screens pinned in space with 3DoF head tracking, controlled from the iPhone as a trackpad and keyboard.
- Full Screen, Video view and packed 3D for web video.
- Spatial Player for Apple spatial photos and videos, side-by-side 3D, and 180° and 360° video from Photos or Files.
- Xbox Cloud Gaming and GeForce NOW with a paired controller.
- Ad blocking with a built-in list, your own filter lists and rules, per-site switch, and backup to a file.
- Home page with editable shortcuts.
- Protected video with Apple FairPlay, with per-site Safari identity and desktop site switches for streaming services.
- Drop-down menus on web pages open inside the page, so the pointer can choose an option.
