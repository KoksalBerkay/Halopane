# Code of conduct

Halopane is a small volunteer project. Everyone taking part is expected to:

- Treat other people with respect.
- Keep criticism about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats and sharing someone's private information are not acceptable here.

If something goes wrong, email the maintainer at koksalberkaydenktas@gmail.com. Reports are kept private. The maintainer may remove content or restrict access to keep the project welcoming.
