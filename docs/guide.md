# Halopane guide

The glasses show your workspace. The phone moves the pointer, opens the keyboard and controls playback.

## 🔌 Connect and recenter

Plug the adapter into your iPhone and the glasses into the adapter. Open Halopane and allow Bluetooth. Put on the glasses, look at the spot where you want the workspace, then tap **Recenter** on the phone. The workspace moves together, keeping the space between your screens.

If the top or sides are cut off in Full Screen, open **More (⋯) → Settings → Visible area**. Adjust the edges until you can see the full border and all four corners.

## 📱 Move the pointer

Halopane starts in Phone aim. Open **More → Settings → Input** on the phone to choose a mode.

- **Phone aim.** Point the phone to move the pointer and tap to click. If the aim is off, use **More → Aim again**. Turn the beam off with **Laser pointer in Phone Aim** in Settings.
- **Trackpad.** Slide one finger to move the pointer, tap to click and drag two fingers to scroll. Tap with two fingers for a right click on pages that handle it. Hold two fingers still to recenter.
- **Gaze + tap (experimental).** Look at what you want to select, then tap the phone.

## 🖥️ Open and arrange screens

Tap **+** on the phone or in a screen's toolbar. You can open up to four screens. Adding one keeps the pointer on your current screen. Tap a numbered chip on the phone to switch; in Phone aim or Gaze + tap you can point at another screen and tap to select it.

To move a screen, look where you want it and choose **Place screen here** in its toolbar menu or by holding its chip on the phone. **Close screen** closes it. Use **Undo close** from the phone menu if you closed one by mistake.

Under **Settings → Anchoring**, choose **World-fixed** to leave screens in place, **Follow** to bring them back toward your view when you turn away, or **Reclined** to use your head's tilt when you recenter.

## ⛶ Fill the glasses with one screen

Press **Full Screen** on the phone or in the glasses toolbar. The screen follows your view and the phone becomes a trackpad. Move the pointer to the top of the view to show the hidden toolbar. Press the phone's Full Screen button again to return the screen to its place. Recenter is unavailable while Full Screen is on.

## ⌨️ Type an address or fill in a form

Select the address bar in the glasses, or tap **Keyboard** on the phone when it is shown. Type an address or a search and submit it. The draft appears in the glasses. To change where searches go, use **Settings → Search engine**.

Select a text field in a page to open the phone keyboard. The part of the trackpad above the keyboard still works. If a sign-in needs the phone itself, use **Use sign-in on iPhone** when offered, then tap **Return to glasses** after signing in.

## 🎬 Watch web video

When the app detects a video, the playback controls appear on the phone. Use them to pause, seek or mute, depending on what the video allows. **Video view** gives the video more space. **Exit video view** returns to the page. Some sites have their own fullscreen controls and may behave differently.

### ✨ Turn 2D video into 3D

Tap **3D** in the playback controls. Halopane opens the video in Video view and Full Screen, then adds depth that it estimates on the iPhone from each frame. Switch the glasses to 3D with their physical control when prompted; depth appears as soon as they are in 3D mode. The first time takes a few seconds while the depth model prepares.

While 3D is on, the phone shows a **Depth** slider and a **Pop-out** switch. Depth sets how strong the effect is. With Pop-out off, the picture sits behind the screen; with it on, near objects come towards you. Changes show in the glasses straight away and are remembered. Tap **3D** again to go back to 2D.

Video protected with FairPlay, such as on Netflix or Disney+, can't be converted: the system doesn't let apps read its picture. Halopane says so and keeps the video in 2D.

### Video that is already 3D

For a video that already packs two eye views into one frame, hold **3D** in the playback controls and pick the layout that matches the video: **Side by side** or **Half side by side**. Over-under layouts are available in Spatial Player for local files. Use the glasses' physical 3D control when prompted. **Swap eyes** reverses the two views, and **Turn 3D off** returns to the ordinary video.

### Streaming services

Services that protect video with Apple FairPlay can play, the same way they do in Safari. Halopane already presents itself as Safari to known streaming sites, and opens Netflix, Disney+, Hulu, Prime Video and Peacock as desktop sites. If another service says your browser is not supported or the video never starts, open the screen's toolbar menu and try **Identify as Safari**, then **Use desktop site**. Both are remembered per site.

## 🗂️ Open Photos or Files

Choose **Open Photos** or **Open Files** from Home or **More** on the phone, then select an item on the phone. Documents and ordinary photos and videos open in a browser screen. Recognised spatial or panoramic media opens in Spatial Player. Use **Open in Spatial Player** from the phone menu for a photo or video already open in a screen. For Office files where the layout matters, open a PDF export.

## 🥽 Use Spatial Player

Set **View** to **Flat**, **180°** or **360°** to match the media. **Layout** describes how the eyes are stored: **Mono**, one of the side-by-side or over-under choices, or **Spatial stereo** when the file contains separate eye views. Switch the glasses to 3D using their physical control when prompted.

For an ordinary 2D photo or video, tap **3D** next to Layout to add depth, the same way as for web video. 3D stays on as you open the next item until you turn it off.

Tap the phone surface to play or pause. Drag to scrub a flat video or to look around in a panorama. Use **Recenter** to reset the view or **World-fixed** to pin a flat view in place when that option is available. Use **Open in screen** to switch to the browser viewer or **Close player** to return to your workspace.

## 🏠 Organise Home

Use the toolbar's Home button to open Home. To save the current page as a shortcut, choose **Add to Home** from the toolbar menu. On the phone, open **Settings → Organize Home shortcuts** to add, rename, move or remove shortcuts. Use **Edit** to reorder them. Use **History** in a screen's toolbar menu to revisit pages or clear your history.

## 🛡️ Adjust ad blocking

Open **More (⋯) → Ad blocking** on the phone to toggle lists, add a list or edit **My rules**. Use a screen's toolbar menu to change blocking for just that site. See the [ad blocking guide](ad-blocking.md) for supported rules and how to back up your settings.

## 🧭 Recover tracking

If screens move out of place, look where you want them and tap **Recenter**. If tracking is paused, give it a moment to return. If it stays paused, try **More → Restart tracking**. If that doesn't help, close and reopen the app.

If the picture is stuck but tracking still works, try **More → Fix display**. For a bug report, attach the report from **Settings → Troubleshooting → Share report**.
